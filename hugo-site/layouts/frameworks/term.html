MITRE ATLAS (Adversarial Threat Landscape for Artificial-Intelligence Systems) is a knowledge base of adversarial tactics and techniques targeting AI/ML systems, maintained by MITRE Corporation. Structured analogously to the well-known MITRE ATT&CK framework, it covers 101 techniques across 16 tactics — with expansions into agentic AI attacks, RAG-based attacks, and AI supply chain threats.

This page tracks how ATLAS techniques appear in our published threat intelligence. Use it to understand which attack vectors are active, which are emerging, and how they relate to each other.

→ Official MITRE ATLAS site

{{ else }}

The OWASP LLM Top 10 is a standard awareness document for developers and security practitioners covering the most critical security risks in Large Language Model applications, published by the Open Worldwide Application Security Project (OWASP).

This page tracks how these 10 risk categories appear across our published articles, revealing which vulnerabilities the industry is discovering and discussing most actively.

→ Official OWASP LLM Top 10
